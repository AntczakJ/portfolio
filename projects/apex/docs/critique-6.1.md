## Biggest risk

The portfolio thesis is "I can ship a real, performant, art-directed WebGL configurator." Engineering half delivered; the object inside the canvas was a toy, the stills a different toy, the framing exposed the underside, the dark stage contradicted its theme. Fixing the object (real GLB) + framing + theme-aware stage closes the gap.

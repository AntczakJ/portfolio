- [x] **Task 0.4 — ADR-005 authored (`architect`, 2026-05-29).** Persistence schema for footprint cells and tick archive. **Tick archive: option 2** — plain Postgres + native declarative monthly partitioning on `ticks(ts_ms)`, retention horizon **30 days** of raw ticks (drop-partition is O(1), covers the demo-pitch replay surface, keeps year-1 worst-case hot storage under ~50 GB inside Fly Managed Postgres standard volume). **Cells: option 6** — single `footprint_cells` table, composite PK on `(symbol, bucket_ts, price_bucket)`, write-through from the Rust worker on bar-close, _no partitioning_ at v1 scale (year-1 cell volume ~26 M rows is below the ~50 M-row partition-management break-even). **Cells kept indefinitely** per ADR-004's "closed-bar history is the durable source of truth" invariant. **Write path:** Elysia writes ticks directly to Postgres (batched at the 50 ms coalescing window via `COPY ticks FROM STDIN`, decoupled from worker availability — a worker crash-loop does not block the tick archive).
